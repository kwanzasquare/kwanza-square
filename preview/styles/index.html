Six soldier styles

Each row shows the two armies large, then at 46px, then at 26px on the board's own blue. The 26px strip is the one that decides it — that is the size a thumb actually sees. Everything here is SVG drawn from maths; there are no image files, and this page runs no scripts.

1 — Sculpted figure

Martin’s reference done properly: sloped shoulders, a waist, limbs that taper into wrist and ankle. Soft and human. The closest to the photograph.

large  ·  46px  ·  26px on the board

2 — Carved wood

Senufo and Baule statuary: elongated, columnar, a crest on the head, carved bands across the body, standing on a plinth. The most culturally rooted of the six.

large  ·  46px  ·  26px on the board

3 — Cast bronze

Benin-inspired: banded collar, headdress, ornamented chest, spear held at the side. Ceremonial and heavy — the metal gradient does most of the work.

large  ·  46px  ·  26px on the board

4 — Bold silhouette

No interior detail at all — one flat shape and a rim. Easily the most readable of the six on a small phone, and the coldest.

large  ·  46px  ·  26px on the board

5 — Low-poly facets

Built from flat triangular facets, each a slightly different shade, the way a modern game renders carved stone. Sharp rather than soft.

large  ·  46px  ·  26px on the board

6 — Turned base

A person from the waist up, a weighted turned base below — like a chess piece. Unmistakably a game piece that sits on a board, and still a figure.

large  ·  46px  ·  26px on the board
